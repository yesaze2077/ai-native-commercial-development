## Hypothesis and acceptance

Route new products through evidence-backed product decisions, experience/visual contracts, architecture decisions and a runnable vertical slice, then acceptance, launch and product feedback. Existing-product changes enter only affected stages and reuse accepted artifacts. This should close lifecycle handoff gaps without making narrow tasks run a full waterfall.

Required outcomes: concise entrypoint with explicit routing; self-contained optional guides; existing templates extended rather than duplicate truths; a deterministic local evidence consistency validator with negative tests; no default technology stack or tool purchase; actual local repository exercises plus independent current scenario responses and review. A manifest cannot authenticate execution, reviewers, approvals, market demand or commercial quality.
