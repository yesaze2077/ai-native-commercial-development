Installation establishes that the update was applied, not that it improved productivity. One apparently faster task cannot establish causation or a general saving; task complexity, environment, cache state and concurrent changes may differ.

Link later observations to the same pattern and installed version. Record improved, regressed or unknown outcomes with the actual evidence and limits, retaining comparable duration/rework/prompting/check-queue data where available. A positive outcome is not another failure occurrence. Missing measurements remain unknown; do not invent a percentage improvement.

The new regression is adverse evidence and reopens review of the cause, scope and current rule. Reproduce it and prepare a narrowly justified correction or rollback proposal, with exact diff/version, affected checks and the recovery/acceptance plan. Existing project work may be corrected within its own authorization, but the ledger cannot automatically revert the active skill. Obtain the user's confirmation of the concrete rule change/rollback, then verify the authorized result and preserve the history. Old applied status and a test score do not authorize automatic rollback.
