The readiness audit is incomplete until release evidence is established. Quick checks and cached integration results are useful local evidence, not commercial acceptance or production authorization.

On the exact candidate, freshly execute all declared release-stage checks, including declared quick/integration commands, plus project-required protected CI/build/type/security/dependency checks and applicable critical journeys, independent review, commercial promises, migration/backup/restore and recovery gates. Configure inner commands to satisfy full/fresh policy too; a wrapper cannot turn an internally affected-only command into full coverage.

Inspect the actual revision/configuration/environment, residual risks, monitoring/abort and rollback/forward-fix readiness, and the human ship decision. Deployment receipt and fresh smoke belong to the actual authorized deployed environment, never to the local cache. Mark missing evidence pending/failed and return the corresponding readiness recommendation. The user's audit request is read-only; planner/cache green cannot authorize production actions, automatic deployment or rollback.
